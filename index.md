I'm an assistant professor in the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research and the Department of Biostatistics at Peking University. 

Previously, I received my M.S. in Mathematics at Tsinghua University. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics at Tsinghua University, where I was advised by Dr. Lin Hou. I was a postdoctoral research fellow in Department of Statistics and Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at Stanford University, working with Dr. Wing Hung Wong and Dr. Alexander Eckehart Urban.

Because off-the-shelf methods often fall short of capturing the complexity of human biology, I develop tailored statistical and machine learning/AI methods to address challenging questions in human genetics and genomics. My current research spans three interconnected areas: (1) cross-trait and cross-population association analysis using genome-wide association study (GWAS) data, (2) genetic risk prediction from high-dimensional data, and (3) integrative analysis of genome sequence and functional genomics data. 

I'm looking for highly motivated students and postdocs with background in mathematics, statistics, biology, genetics, computer science, and AI.
